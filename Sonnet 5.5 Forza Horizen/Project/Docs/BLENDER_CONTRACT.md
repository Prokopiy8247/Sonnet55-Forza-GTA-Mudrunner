## 1. Shared-Blender etiquette (several agents use the SAME live Blender session)
* Every `execute_blender_code` call must be **self-contained and atomic**: do not rely on selection/active object/mode left by an earlier call; set what you need inside the call. Keep each call < ~20 s.
* Only create/modify/delete objects, meshes, collections and materials **belonging to your own asset** (name prefix = asset id, e.g. `car01_hatch_pulse_*`). Never touch another asset's data. Shared materials (`M_*`, see §4) are created through `ensure_material()` (idempotent) and may be *read* by anyone but only *edited* by the kit owner.
* Forbidden: `bpy.ops.wm.read_*`, `wm.open_mainfile`, `wm.revert_mainfile`, `wm.read_homefile`, `bpy.data.orphans_purge`, deleting collections you do not own, changing scene units/render settings, hiding/excluding collections you do not own.
* `bpy.ops.wm.save_mainfile()` may be called by anyone after your asset is in a consistent state (calls are atomic). Never "save as" elsewhere.
* Every asset is generated by an **idempotent script stored in the project** (`Tools/blender/...`) executed in Blender via `exec(compile(open(path,encoding='utf8').read(), path, 'exec'))`. Re-running it removes the asset's previous objects first and rebuilds. Do NOT paste giant scripts only into MCP calls: the script file is the source of truth.
* Staging: each asset sits at its own world position so `look` with `target=[root]` frames only it. Cars row: `x = index*12 m, y = 0` (index 1..10). Traffic row: `y = 30 m`. Props/kits: `y = 60+ m`. Collection of the asset is a child of `FORZA_UE/Cars|Traffic|Props|Settlements|Roadside|Nature|Roads|Bridges_Tunnels`.
* Visual QA with `look` (mode `angles`, `target=[<root>]`, shading `material` or `rendered`, `max_size` ≤ 768 for routine checks). Revise until it looks like a believable, finished model — not boxes. Take 4 views minimum (front-3/4, side, rear-3/4, top) and a close-up of details.

## 2. Units & axes
* 1 Blender unit = 1 m. Z up. **Vehicles: +X forward, +Y left, +Z up.** (UE will show forward = +X, left = −Y; the FBX export recipe handles the handedness.)
* Car-space origin: ground plane under the **middle of the wheelbase**, on the centreline: tyres touch z = 0 at rest.
* Apply scale/rotation (Ctrl-A equivalent) on all exported meshes. No negative scales. Normals outward, no loose geometry, no n-gons on curved surfaces that will be triangulated badly.

## 5. Export (script `Tools/blender/lib/fbx_export.py`, recipe verified: 4 m body → 400 uu, X forward preserved, left side → −Y in UE)
* Output folder `Exports/<Category>/<asset_id>/` inside the project (not committed to Git except `.json`; the FBX are regenerated, but imported `.uasset` ARE committed).
* **One FBX per part**, part moved to the world origin for the export (restored afterwards): `<id>_Body.fbx` (Body + its `UCX_*` hulls), `<id>_Glass.fbx`, `<id>_Interior.fbx`, `<id>_SteeringWheel.fbx` (centred on the hub), `<id>_Wheel_FL.fbx` … `_RR.fbx` (centred on the axle). This guarantees correct pivots in UE regardless of import options.
* Axis: `axis_forward='-Y'`, `axis_up='Z'`, `apply_scale_options='FBX_SCALE_ALL'`, `object_types={'MESH','EMPTY'}`, `mesh_smooth_type='FACE'`, `use_tspace=True`, `add_leaf_bones=False`, `bake_anim=False`.
* Sidecar `Exports/Cars/<id>/<id>.json` (written by the same script): `{ "id", "display_name", "units":"m", "dimensions":{length,width,height}, "wheelbase", "track_front", "track_rear", "wheels":{"FL":{"center":[x,y,z],"radius":r,"width":w}, …}, "steering_wheel":{"center":[..],"axis":[..]}, "cameras":{"hood":[..],"cockpit":[..],"bumper":[..]}, "lights":{"head_l":[..],…}, "exhaust":[..], "material_slots":{part:[names]}, "tri_counts":{part:n} }` — all positions in **Blender car-space metres** (the importer converts: UE x = x·100, UE y = −y·100, UE z = z·100).

## 6. Quality bar (asset-quality gate)
Believable proportions (wheel/body ratios, correct overhangs, belt-line, A-pillar rake), consistent panel flow with subtle surface curvature (subdivision/bevel), visible detail (door/hood/trunk shut lines, handles, mirrors, grille, headlight/tail-light units, bumpers, exhaust, number plate recesses, wheel arches with clearance, tyre sidewall/tread, rim spokes, brake discs, interior seats/dash/steering wheel/pedals). Distinct silhouettes between the 10 cars. No intersecting z-fighting faces, no inside-out normals, no stray default `Cube` objects.

## 7. Non-vehicle assets (nature, roadside, settlements, bridges/tunnels pieces)
* Script per kit: `Tools/blender/<category>/<kit>.py` (idempotent), staging row `y = 60 + 20*categoryIndex` (nature y=60, roadside y=80, settlements y=100, bridges/tunnels y=120), objects spaced 8-25 m apart along +X so `look` with `target=[asset]` frames only one.
* Origin = **base centre on the ground (z = 0)**, +Z up, +X = "front" (door side / sign face / gate opening). Pieces that tile (guard rail segment, fence panel, wall) have origin at the start of the segment on its centreline and extend along **+X** by a documented length (e.g. 4.0 m guard rail, 2.5 m fence panel).
* Export: **one FBX per asset** into `Exports/<Category>/<AssetId>.fbx` (+ `<AssetId>.json` with dimensions, tri counts, material slots, recommended collision (`none|box|convex|complex_simple`), biome tags, scale range, LOD info, wind/WPO hint (`trunk`,`leaf` vertex-colour mask)). Use `fbx_export.export_fbx(..., center_on="self")`.
* Collision: `UCX_<AssetId>_NN` hulls for solid things (rocks, trunks as capsule-like hull, buildings, signs' posts); none for foliage canopies and grass.
* Nanite friendly: opaque materials, real geometry for leaves (card clusters with alpha **masked**, but prefer modelled leaf clumps for Nanite foliage), vertex colours for wind (R = branch flex, G = leaf flutter, B = AO) on trees/bushes/grass.
* LODs/Nanite: rely on Nanite for opaque meshes; for masked foliage provide a lightweight billboard-cross/impostor card mesh `<AssetId>_Far` (2 quads) as optional separate FBX.
* Triangle budgets: tree hero 20-60 k, bush 3-8 k, rock 2-10 k, building 5-40 k, sign/post 0.2-2 k, guard rail segment 0.5-1.5 k.
* Variants: at least 3 variants per tree/rock family (different seeds/params in the same script), distinct silhouettes per biome (conifer, oak, birch, palm, cypress/scrub, dead desert tree, cactus saguaro/barrel, boulders, cliff slabs, mesas pieces optional).
* Materials: kit-specific `M_<Kit>_<Name>` (≤ 8 per asset) with plausible Principled colours so the Blender previews already look right; UE side recreates them as master-material instances by NAME.
