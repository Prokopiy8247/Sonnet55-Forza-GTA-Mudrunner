## 2. Conventions (all three cars)

* 1 BU = 1 m, Z up, **+X forward, +Y left (LHD)**; car-space origin on the ground under the middle of the wheelbase. All meshes have applied transforms, identity parent inverse, no modifiers; `<id>_ROOT` carries the contract custom properties (`wheelbase`, `track_*`, `wheel_radius_*`, `wheel_width_*` (measured tyre width), `ride_height`, `length`, `width`, `height`, `drivetrain`, `steering_side`).
* One FBX per part, each exported centred on its own pivot (Body/Glass/Interior on the car origin; SteeringWheel on the hub; wheels on the axle). FBX recipe from `fbx_export.py` (4 m body -> 400 uu, left side -> -Y in UE). Import with **Combine Meshes off, Import Materials off, Auto Generate Collision off**; the `UCX_*` hulls in `_Body.fbx` become the simple collision of the body. The Body FBX carries tangents + binormals for both UV channels (every mesh is tris / quads only, no n-gons).
* Material slots are the contract names; map them to the master-material instances **by name**. `M_Glass` appears only in `_Glass`; the lamp slots (`M_Headlight`, `M_Taillight`, `M_Reverse`, `M_Indicator`) only in `_Body`. `M_Paint2` is the accent slot: Pulse roof pack + pillars + spoiler, Thunderline stripes; the Cadence body uses `M_Paint` only. Nanite: all parts except `_Glass` are opaque.
* Right-hand parts are mirror-built (no negative scales); wheels FR / RR are mirror-built so the spokes face outward. Normals: smooth custom normals from the lofted shell (`apply_shell_normals`), hard edges above 62 deg.

## 6. Fix round 1 (art-director findings) - what changed

* **Cadence export was stale (blocker)**: the Body had been rebuilt after the 23:26 export (FBX 65.3 k Body tris / 18-vertex UCX_04 / old anchor positions against 62.7 k / 16 / new positions in the scene). The car was rebuilt from the script, validated and re-exported; the JSON now carries the live anchors (tail lamp, reverse, head lamp) and tri counts. `export_car_checked` (in `carkit_ext_fix_01_02_03.py`) re-reads every written FBX and fails if the tangent / binormal layers are missing or the file is older than the call; all three cars were exported with it. Independent proof: `Tools/blender/qa/fbx_geometry_info.py` reads the binary FBX back - Body vertices / corners (Pulse 29 834 / 126 022, Cadence 33 680 / 141 668, Thunderline 31 813 / 133 786), the wheel meshes and every UCX hull vertex count equal the live scene exactly.
* **Near-zero-area faces**: `cleanup_slivers` welds the vertices of every face below 1e-7 m2 to their neighbours within 0.25 mm, dissolves the degenerate geometry, repairs the non-manifold edges a weld can create and re-triangulates n-gons. Lesson: a post-weld n-gon makes Blender's FBX exporter silently drop the tangent layers (Body FBX shrinks to ~40 %), so the clean-up ends with a beauty triangulation.

## 8. Cross-car known weaknesses

* Interiors, door cards and seats come from the shared kit and look alike; per-car differences are the wheel, seat sport-ness, rear seating, dash height and the small trim additions listed above.
* Preview paint is the shared red `M_Paint`; the paint finishes are intended to be swapped in UE.
* The loft is a dense quad grid (~16 k quads): Body 57 - 65 k triangles, 135 - 140 k per car (limit 150 k).
* ~2 300 open boundary edges in the Pulse / Cadence Body (window reveals, lens patches, liners) are by design (validator warning only).
* Glass is a single shell with a black reveal; no frame thickness, no wiper pivot on the windscreen.
* No damage / deformation mesh, no LOD meshes (Nanite assumed).
