## 2. Conventions (all three cars)

* 1 BU = 1 m, Z up, **+X forward, +Y left (LHD)**; car-space origin on the ground under the middle of the wheelbase. All meshes have applied transforms, identity parent inverse, no modifiers; `<id>_ROOT` carries the contract custom properties (`wheelbase`, `track_*`, `wheel_radius_*`, `wheel_width_*` (measured tyre width), `ride_height`, `length`, `width`, `height`, `drivetrain`, `steering_side`).
* One FBX per part, each exported centred on its own pivot (Body/Glass/Interior on the car origin; SteeringWheel on the hub; wheels on the axle). FBX recipe from `fbx_export.py` (4 m body -> 400 uu, left side -> -Y in UE). Import with **Combine Meshes off, Import Materials off, Auto Generate Collision off**; the `UCX_*` hulls in `_Body.fbx` become the simple collision of the body.
* Material slots are the contract names (`M_Paint`, `M_Paint2`, `M_Chrome`, `M_TrimBlack`, ... `M_Exhaust`); map them to the master-material instances **by name**. `M_Glass` appears only in `_Glass`. `M_Paint2` is the accent slot: Thunderline stripes, Vesper ducktail + mirror caps; the Cadence body uses `M_Paint` only. Nanite: all parts except `_Glass` are opaque.
* Right-hand parts are mirror-built (no negative scales); wheels FR / RR are mirror-built so the spokes face outward. Normals: smooth custom normals from the lofted shell (`apply_shell_normals`), hard edges above 62 deg.

## 6. QA record (honest iteration log)

Every car went through the same loop: build -> `look` (4 angles, rendered / material, close-ups of lamps, grille, hood, wheels, cockpit) -> `zebra_car` reflection-stripe checks (side, front 3/4, rear 3/4) -> validate -> fix -> repeat.

* **Cadence** - it0 first loft (blobby tail corners, thick A-pillar, headlamp pocket ragged, bumper valance patch with vertical edges); it1 wrapped black bumper band that ends at the arches, shoulder crease + tumblehome, wider plan corners, A-pillar moved forward, headlamp bezel; it2 enlarged wrap tail lamps, hood loop with filleted front corner, found + fixed the kit-pattern bug that put the right arch mouldings on the left side (clearance left/right now symmetric 22 mm), rolled arch lips; it3 UV / validate / export.
* **Thunderline** - it0 first build (uncut grille: the skin-following pocket cap left the dome of the nose uncut -> new `pocket_cutter_flat`), it1 flat grille / lamp / tail recesses with flat-referenced assemblies, wider nose corners and stripes; it2 hood shut-line loop, scoop rebuilt (first version faced backwards, then too blocky -> forward wedge with chrome intake bars), quarter scoop + trim loops, interior mirror re-positioned (poked through the screen); it3 wheel-arch clearance QA (tracks 1.60 / 1.58, `zw` 0.66 -> tyres 7-11 mm inside the fender lip), arch lips, final validate / export.
* **Vesper** - it0 first build, it1 bumper valance raised so every pocket sits inside the black band (no ragged boundary), interior mirror fixed, ducktail resized; it2 wheel-arch clearance (front tyres stuck out 19 mm -> track 1.54 / 1.53, tyres 235 / 275, hwm 0.935, tumblehome 5 cm, `zw` 0.64 -> +8 / +13 mm), black window frame loop, removed the ragged corner intakes, arch lips; it3 UV / validate / export.
* Note for the kit owner: `car01_hatch_pulse.py` (and the recipe in `Docs/CARKIT_API.md`) mirrors the right-hand arch mouldings with `mirrored_copy(sw) if cy < 0`, but `arch_moulding` is already swept on the right side with the ray (0, 1, 0), so the mirror moves the strip to the LEFT (left arches get two mouldings, right arches none). The Cadence script uses the right-side sweep directly. car01 was not touched.
* Validation (`validate_car`): all three OK - no loose geometry, no non-manifold edges, no inverted closed islands, contract material names, `UVMap` overlap <= 0.07 %, wheel pivots on the axle with tyres touching z = 0, UCX 4 hulls <= 32 verts and convex, triangle budget 128 k - 140 k. Open boundary edges in the Body are the glass reveals, lens patches and liners (by design).

## 8. Cross-car known weaknesses

* Interiors, door cards and seats come from the shared kit and look alike; only seat sport-ness, rear seats, steering wheel, parcel shelf and dash height differ.
* Preview paint is the shared red `M_Paint`; the paint finishes are intended to be swapped in UE.
* The loft is a dense quad grid (~16 k quads) so the Body is 56 - 65 k triangles; the three cars together are ~406 k triangles of LOD0 (Nanite assumed).
* Glass is a single shell with a black reveal; there is no frame thickness, no sun-visor / mirror stalk geometry on the windscreen and no wiper pivot.
* No damage / deformation mesh, no brake-light emissive variants (UE material handles it), no LOD meshes.
