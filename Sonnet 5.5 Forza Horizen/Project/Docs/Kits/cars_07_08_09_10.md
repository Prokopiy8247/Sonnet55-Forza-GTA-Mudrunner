## 7. Remaining imperfections (honest list)

* **Shared-kit look**: the Ridge cabin (kit dash with binnacle + screen, box seats; only the cage, harnesses and bare panels are specific), the seats of the Ranger / Mule and their shared utility dash come from generic builders; only the Brisa interior is fully car-specific.
* **Doors, hood, tailgate, bed gate, boot are shut lines in one Body mesh**, not separate animatable meshes; there is no engine bay on any car, no suspension geometry behind the arches except the visible axles / springs of the Ranger, Mule and Brisa.
* **Glass** is a thin sheet per window with a black reveal (alpha 0.3 preview, so the cabin always shows in Blender); there are no defroster lines, wiper articulation or B-pillar belt anchors; the Brisa windscreen is a single gently curved pane.
* **UVs** are the kit's smart-project atlases (non-overlapping, uneven texel density); do not bake lighting into `UVMap`; use `UVLivery` for stripes / numbers.
* **Preview colours**: every shared material is the kit default (red paint, dark leather); UE instances must override them (see the intent table above, notably Brisa walnut / leather / canvas).
* **Triangle counts** of the Ranger and Mule sit near the 150 k ceiling (the loft is dense and the block tyres cost ~5.5 k each); a hand-made LOD1 would be needed for non-Nanite platforms.
