## 5. Recommended UE setup
* **Import**: one StaticMesh per FBX (Combine Meshes off, Auto Generate Collision off, Import Materials off - assign the shared master instances by name).
  Body + Interior + wheels + SteeringWheel: **Nanite enabled**; Glass: no Nanite (translucent). Body FBX carries the `UCX_*` hulls - keep them as the
  complex-as-simple replacement for the physics body (do not use per-poly collision). Wheels / interior / glass / steering wheel: **no collision**.
* **Collision / physics**: body = the 3-4 UCX hulls (cab + hood + bed for the Mule, tub + bonnet + two wing lumps for the Brisa). Wheels are driven by
  the vehicle wheel setup from the JSON (radius, width, centre), not by their meshes. Suggested starting masses 2150 / 2350 / 900 kg, centre of mass about
  35-40 % of the height (Ranger 0.75 m, Mule 0.80 m, Brisa 0.45 m above the ground, slightly forward of the wheelbase middle).
* **Pivots**: Body / Glass / Interior at the car origin (wheelbase middle, ground), wheels at the axle centre (spoke face outwards), steering wheel at the hub.
  Place the wheel components at the JSON centres converted with UE x = x*100, y = -y*100, z = z*100.
* **Scale**: keep 1.0 (player cars). Tolerance for variants 0.97-1.03 uniform; never non-uniform (wheel/arch clearance is tight: 6 cm Ranger / Mule, ~8 cm Brisa).
  These are player-car assets (LOD0 only, Nanite); traffic vehicles have their own cheaper meshes.
* **Lamps / materials**: `M_Headlight`, `M_Taillight`, `M_Reverse`, `M_Indicator` only exist on the Body (emissive instances driven by brake / reverse / indicator
  state). Brisa uses `M_Taillight` for its two small round tail lamps and `M_Headlight` for the bucket lenses; `M_Indicator` = the two front amber side lamps.
* **Camera**: cockpit anchors are driver-eye positions; the Brisa cockpit view looks over the low windscreen frame (it is intentionally open).

## 6. QA summary
Each car went through at least three build / look / fix rounds (solid + material + rendered with the temporary `qa_studio` lights, wireframe checks,
close-ups of nose, tail, wheels, cockpit; the QA lights were removed again). Fixes made along the way include: right-hand flares were mirrored onto the left
(Ranger), flat end caps instead of domed ones (diagonal shading on the boxy tail), snorkel path, 1 mm boolean slivers (`heal_nonmanifold` + `clean_tiny`),
flare sweeps that missed the shell (Mule), cab/bed length and bumper overhang, red hood cap visible through the Brisa grille, boot straps, rear plate seat,
lamp-slot rule of kit rev 3 (gauge needles on `M_Caliper`), wire-wheel width (sidecar now reports 0.123 m = tyre width).

## 7. Known weaknesses
* **Interior** of Ranger and Mule is the kit's generic cockpit (sport dash with binnacle and screen, kit seats); only floor, lining, cargo floor and positions are
  car-specific. The steering-wheel hub trim is plain grey. The Brisa interior is custom but has no stitched UV detail (texture it in UE with the `M_IntSeat` leather).
* **Doors, hood, tailgate, bed gate, boot are shut lines in one Body mesh**, not separate animatable meshes. The Mule bed wheel boxes are decorative (the tyre
  never reaches them), and the cab/bed gap shows the shell edges.
* **Glass** is a thin sheet per window (Ranger rear window is cut from the end cap, so its corners are rectangular; the Brisa windscreen is a flat pane in a
  frame). Wipers are small black ribs on the cowl, not on the glass. No defroster lines, no B-pillar belt anchors.
* **Brisa**: tub / bonnet junction at the cowl is a smooth step, not a real firewall; the engine bay is closed (no engine); the soft-top is only the folded boot;
  spokes are 5-sided tubes (shimmer at distance without Nanite / TAA); tyre tread is a simple rib pattern; wing joints to the running boards are overlaps, not welds.
* **Ranger / Mule**: tris are close to the ceiling (137 k / 131 k) because the knobbly tyres cost ~6 k each (can be dropped to 48 segments if needed); bull bar,
  rack and snorkel are plain tubes / boxes; hood vents are cut strips; body side is a fairly flat slab with only a subtle character line.
* **UV layout** is the kit's smart-project (auto islands, non-overlapping, uneven texel density); do not bake lighting into `UVMap`. Use `UVLivery` for stripes.
* **Preview colours**: all shared materials are the kit's defaults (red paint, dark leather); the Brisa wood trim shows as grey in Blender - UE instances must override it.
* The sidecar `width` of the Ranger / Mule is the fender (flare) width without mirrors (1.922 / 1.980 m); roster targets were 1.92 / 1.96 m.
