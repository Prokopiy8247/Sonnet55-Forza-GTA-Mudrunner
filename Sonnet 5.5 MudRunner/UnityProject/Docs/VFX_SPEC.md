# VFX_SPEC — particles, water surface, weather visuals, vehicle dirt (contract for the VFX worker)

Unity 6000.6, URP 17.6, C#, hand-written HLSL where needed. All textures procedural (no downloaded assets). Must be cheap: pooled particle systems, no per-frame allocation, effects only near the camera/player vehicles.

## 1. Read first
`Vehicles/Vehicle.cs`, `Wheel.cs` (per wheel: `grounded`, `slipVelocity`, `slipRatio`, `sinkage`, `load`, `soil.dominant`/`soil.wet`, `patchCenter`, `contactNormal`, `omega`, `churn`), `VehicleWater.cs` (`inWater`, `submergedFraction`, `floating`, `currentSpeed`), `Drivetrain.cs` (`rpm`, `engineTorque`, `loadFraction`, `engineOn`, `clutchTorque`), `Ground/WaterMap.cs`, `GroundService` (`wetness`), `Atmosphere/AtmosphereController.cs` (rain, hour, overcast), `Rendering/` (ground shader: `_MR_Wetness`, `_MR_MudField`).

## 2. Deliverables (`Assets/Game/Runtime/Vfx/`, `Assets/Game/Shaders/Vfx/`, editor capture harness `Assets/Editor/Vfx/`, tests)
1. **WheelVfx** (one per vehicle, created by `VfxDirector` for every `Vehicle`, including trailers): per wheel
   * mud/dirt spray: chunky brown particles thrown rearwards/upwards, emission ∝ spin slip velocity × softness (shallow/deep mud/bog), colour from the surface tint; ballistic, collide-free (cheap), fade; heavier chunks that land and vanish.
   * dust on dry firm/gravel/sand surfaces at speed (soft, pale, drifting), pebbles on gravel;
   * water spray/wake when the wheel is in water (white, fine) and splash bursts on entering mud/water above ~2 m/s;
   * a short "mud slap" burst when the wheel digs hard.
2. **BodyVfx**: bow wave + side foam sheets and turbulence at the hull when `VehicleWater.inWater` (scaled by speed and depth), exhaust smoke (diesel puff on throttle tip-in and load, thin black on heavy load, white when cold/stalled-restart; position from the model's `Exhaust` anchor or a def-based point), engine-flooded steam/splutter puff, brake dust optional.
3. **Wheel/vehicle dirt**: `VehicleDirt` component raising a 0..1 dirt level with time in mud (per surface wetness/softness), washing off in water; drives a shared shader property so the lower body and wheels turn brown. Implement with a small hand-written URP shader `MudRunner/VehicleLit` (copy URP Lit structure; add dirt blend by world-space height above the wheel hubs + noise) used by the vehicle materials in the prefab builder, OR a MaterialPropertyBlock-based tint of the existing project materials if the shader is too costly. Document the choice.
4. **Rain/weather visuals** (`WeatherVfx`, listens to `AtmosphereController.rain`): camera-following rain streaks (particle system or a GPU quad strip shader) with intensity, wind slant, splashes on the ground near the camera (pooled), mist/fog thickening is handled by AtmosphereController; windscreen droplets are optional (cockpit camera). Lightning is optional.
5. **Water surface rendering** (`WaterRenderer` + shader `MudRunner/Water`): builds chunked meshes from `GroundMap.water` (only where the surface is above the ground), flowing normal maps along the current direction, depth-based colour/foam (use URP depth texture; enable via the pipeline asset if required and document), reflections via the sky/reflection probe, shore foam, refraction using `_CameraOpaqueTexture` (document the URP asset settings you need - you may edit `Assets/Settings/PC_RPAsset.asset` through an editor script). It must replace the simple placeholder `WaterSurfaceMesh` of the world worker (coordinate by checking `Assets/Game/Runtime/World/`). Chunks beyond ~600 m disabled.
6. **VfxDirector** (MonoBehaviour created by GameBootstrap/GameManager via a static `Install()`; also auto-attaches to every new `Vehicle`), quality levels (low/med/high scale particle counts), pooled objects, a global on/off toggle for the debug menu (`VfxDirector.Enabled`).
7. Verification: capture harness (`GFX=1 Tools/ub.sh <name> MudRunner.EditorTools.VfxCapture.Run`) rendering several scenes to `Captures/vfx_*.png` (a vehicle spinning in deep mud close-up, driving through a ford, rain at dusk, exhaust at full load, dirty vs clean vehicle) + PlayMode tests (no exceptions, pool sizes bounded, no per-frame allocations measured with `GC.GetTotalMemory` deltas in a steady state). LOOK at the PNGs and iterate.

8. **Sky and lighting polish** (`SkyController`, drives/extends `AtmosphereController` without breaking its API): a skybox material (`Skybox/Procedural` or a small hand-written URP sky shader) with sun disc, horizon haze and a dusk/night palette tied to `AtmosphereController.hour/overcast/rain`; stars at night; a simple cloud layer (scrolling procedural cloud quad dome or a shader) that thickens with overcast/rain; ambient/fog colour curves; make the headlight spot lights of `VehicleLights` look right at night (cookie-less soft spots, volumetric-fake optional); `RenderSettings.skybox` must be set by code at game start (the Game scene is tiny and generated by `GameSceneBuilder`); ensure `AtmosphereController` exists in the running game (create it in your `VfxDirector.Install()` if `AtmosphereController.Instance == null`, with `sun = RenderSettings.sun`).

## 3. Rules
`python -I Tools/qc.py` after each C# edit; Unity only via `Tools/ub.sh` / `Tools/rt.sh` (project lock; other workers use Unity). Own folders only; additive hooks in GameBootstrap/GameManager allowed. No git commit; no ProjectSettings/manifest edits except the URP asset settings needed for the water (document them). Don't touch Blender.
